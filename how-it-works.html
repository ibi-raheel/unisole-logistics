Home / How It Works

From sign-up to your first load, fast.

No complicated onboarding. Most carriers are set up and booking freight within 24-48 hours.

1

Sign Up

Complete our quick carrier form. Tell us your equipment, home base, preferred lanes, and rate goals.

2

Send Documents

Send your MC/DOT authority, certificate of insurance, and W-9. We handle every broker setup from there.

3

We Find Loads

Your dedicated dispatcher searches boards and our network, negotiates the rate, and sends you the details.

4

Drive & Get Paid

You approve, roll out, and deliver. We handle the paperwork and line up your next load before you're empty.

What we need from you

A short onboarding, then we take it from here.

  • ✓ Active MC & DOT authority
  • ✓ Certificate of insurance (COI)
  • ✓ Signed W-9
  • ✓ Notice of Assignment (if factoring)
  • ✓ Your preferred lanes & home time
