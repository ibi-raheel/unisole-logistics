Our Story

We know the road, and the paperwork behind it.

UniSole Logistics started because too many hardworking owner-operators were losing money to lowball loads, endless broker calls, and paperwork that never ended. We set out to change that.

Today we run a full dispatch operation for owner-operators and small fleets across the country, sourcing better freight, negotiating stronger rates, and handling the back office so drivers can do what they do best: drive.

We're not a load board and we're not a mega-carrier. We're your dispatch team, and we only succeed when your truck is profitable.
