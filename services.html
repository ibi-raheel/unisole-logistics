Home / Services

Full-service dispatching, handled end to end.

From the first phone call to final payment, UniSole runs the back office so you can run the miles.

Load Sourcing & Planning

We work the load boards and our direct broker relationships 24/7 to build efficient, high-paying trips on the lanes you prefer. Your dispatcher plans loads back-to-back to eliminate empty miles.

Rate Negotiation

Our dispatchers know the market rate on every lane and negotiate hard on your behalf. You get the top dollar the freight will bear, never a lowball rate just to fill the truck.

Broker Setup & Packets

New broker? We complete carrier packets, submit your documents, and get you set up fast so nothing slows down your next booking.

Broker Credit Screening

Before you haul, we check broker credit scores and payment history so you only run for brokers who actually pay.

Factoring Coordination

Already factoring? We coordinate paperwork with your factoring company so invoices go out clean and you get paid faster.

Detention & TONU Recovery

Stuck at a dock or a load canceled last minute? We document and chase detention and Truck-Order-Not-Used pay so your time is compensated.

Paperwork & Documents

Rate confirmations, BOLs, and load documents organized and filed for you, everything in one place, always ready.

24/7 Dispatch Support

Real dispatchers on call any hour, reroutes, breakdowns, or a load falling through. You're never left figuring it out alone.
